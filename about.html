ABOUT THE COUNCIL

Built with students.
For every student.

Sri Vishnu for Students General Council is a student-first campaign focused on practical representation, open communication, and visible follow-through.

The council should be easy to reach and clear about what happens next. Every student deserves a voice in the conversation, a place to bring an idea, and a fair chance to see that idea move forward.
