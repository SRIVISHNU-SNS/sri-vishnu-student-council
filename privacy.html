YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy
Policy.

We keep this simple: we collect only what we need to hear from students, review applications, and run the council's membership features.

01

Information we collect

When you join the team, we may collect your first name, email address, section, and mobile number. If you contact us directly, we also receive the information you include in your message.

02

How we use it

We use this information to review student applications, contact applicants about council opportunities, maintain membership records, issue membership codes, and respond to questions. We do not sell your personal information.

03

Membership records

Approved members receive a membership code and verification page. The public verification page is designed to show limited membership details only, such as the member's first name, section, status, and approval date.

04

Storage and access

Application and membership information is stored in the council's application database. Access to the review dashboard is restricted to authorized council administrators. Please do not submit passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information through the join form.

05

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or remove information you have submitted, subject to records we need to keep for legitimate council administration. To make a request, email srivishnusnsct@gmail.com.

06

Updates

We may update this policy when the council's services or practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
